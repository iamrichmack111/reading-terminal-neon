# Reading Terminal Neon Wiki

Reading Terminal Neon is a colorful, terminal-first reading practice application for young learners. It keeps the interface simple, uses Enter for read-only activities, and reserves typing for activities that actually practice typing or require an answer.

## Core goals

- Keep reading practice simple enough for an early reader.
- Let a child press **Enter** after reading activities.
- Do not require punctuation or capitalization for child typing exercises.
- Save unfinished work and resume it later.
- Provide a session report card and long-term progress tracking.
