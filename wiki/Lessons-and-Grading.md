# Lessons and Grading

## Lesson choices

The home screen offers several session types:

- Full Lesson
- Reading
- Typing + Spelling
- Words
- Story Time
- Quick Mix

## Child-friendly navigation

Read-only activities use **Enter to continue**. Questions still require an answer. Typing exercises ignore punctuation and capitalization where appropriate for a young learner.

## Session grades

At the end of a session, the application can show:

- Overall grade
- Reading grade
- Typing / spelling grade
- First-try accuracy
- Corrections
- Activities completed
- Stars earned

Grades are based on first-try performance while corrections are still required for answer-based activities.
